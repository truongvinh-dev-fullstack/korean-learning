The lesson clip is described by its creator as “Korean vowels”; its exact spoken sequence is not documented on the source page. Avoid assuming that the recording follows the lesson's printed sequence. Other seeded vocabulary has no audio URL until a recording for the exact word is verified. The seed's repair step replaces only its own old placeholder URLs and leaves user supplied URLs intact.
